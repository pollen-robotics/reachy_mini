# First wake-up

The first time you connect to a robot, the app runs a short guide that checks its motors, camera, microphone and speaker. Stay next to the robot: some steps need you.

The robot remembers when the guide is done, so it only runs once per robot. **Skip** at the top right leaves it at any time.

If a check fails, tap the link under the buttons (for example **It didn't move**): the app shows tips for that problem, with links to the [FAQ](../../troubleshooting.md) and Discord, and **Back to test** to try again.

## 1. Tuck Me In

Put the robot in its sleep position: match it to the orange target on screen. When it's in place, the app shows **I'm tucked in and ready**. Tap **Let's go**.

![Tuck me in](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini/raw/main/docs/assets/mobile_app/wake-1-tuck.png)

## 2. Wake Me Up

Gently scratch the top of the robot's head, or make some noise near it. The robot hears it through its microphone and wakes up.

If nothing happens, tap **Sound doesn't work**.

![Wake me up](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini/raw/main/docs/assets/mobile_app/wake-2-wake.png)

## 3. Meet Me

The robot plays its first animation, and the 3D robot on screen does the same. Check that the real robot moves like the picture, then tap **Yes, it moved**. **Move again** replays it, **It didn't move** opens the tips.

![Meet me](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini/raw/main/docs/assets/mobile_app/wake-3-meet.png)

## 4. See Through My Eyes

The robot opens its eyes and the app shows its camera feed. Tap **I can see it**, or **Camera doesn't work**.

![See through my eyes](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini/raw/main/docs/assets/mobile_app/wake-4-eyes.png)

## 5. Hear My Voice

The robot plays a sound. Move the slider until the volume feels right, then tap **Yes, I hear it**. **Play again** replays the sound, **I don't hear it** opens the tips.

![Hear my voice](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini/raw/main/docs/assets/mobile_app/wake-5-voice.png)

## 6. Give Me a Name

Type a name for the robot and tap **Save name**. This step only shows when the robot doesn't have a name yet. You can rename it later in the [settings](using.md#settings).

When everything is done, the app shows **Nice to meet you**, then opens the [session](using.md).
